What the values mean

Eddy keeps every figure separate and labels where it comes from, so an estimate never reads as an official price.

The figures

Catalog price Official

The official current price on Vortex. It is not a sale.

RAP Sales only

The average of recent completed sales only. Never derived from asking prices, estimates, the catalog price or sample data.

Eddy shows RAP only on profiles, as a Limited RAP entry tagged “Unofficial” in the profile's stats row. Today it shows “—”, and its tooltip says why: there is no approved source of completed sales yet.

Estimated value Unofficial

An unofficial estimate, shown separately from RAP and always in italics.

Worth Voltimons

Voltimons' unofficial, RAP-based figure for limited items, shown with its sync time and marked Stale when out of date. Only while Market data is on.

All items · estimated total

A mixed figure: estimates for limited items plus official prices for other items. It is not a sum of official prices.

How Eddy shows them

Complement, don't duplicate

Item pages get no Eddy value: Vortex already shows the price and its own RAP there.

Missing values are never numbers

“Unavailable”, “No estimate”, “Not enough sales” or a “—” whose tooltip gives the reason, never a placeholder number.

Incomplete totals say so

A total with missing items reads “≥ …, incomplete”, with how many items were valued.

Sample data is always marked

Demo values appear only in the popup's labelled demo list, which is off by default.
