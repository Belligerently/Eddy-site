Privacy

Eddy collects nothing about you. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, accounts, backend or advertising. What it reads on Vortex pages is used in your browser and sent nowhere. Only Market data, which is off by default, makes network requests: to Voltimons, which receives what is listed below.

Permissions the extension asks for

storage

Eddy's settings and, only after you turn on Market data, the downloaded market data (item IDs and numbers only) and its refresh schedule. Kept in this browser profile; not synced. Turning Market data off deletes the downloaded data (a failed delete is retried); only the schedule's times are kept.

Access to playvortex.io

To add Eddy's values on catalog and profile pages, an Eddy tab in Vortex's Settings and an “Eddy settings” entry in its account menu. Browsers list this as access to that site.

Nothing else

No host permissions, and no tabs, cookies, scripting, webRequest or identity.

Network requests

While Market data (Voltimons) is off, the default, Eddy makes no requests at all.

While it is on, only Eddy's background worker makes requests, and only to two addresses: https://api.voltimons.io/items and https://api.voltimons.io/stats. It asks when a Vortex page needs data, at most once every 15 minutes. After a failure it waits 15 minutes, then 30, 60 and 120 minutes, then 6 hours. The extension's security policy blocks every other connection.

What Voltimons receives. As with any website visit, Voltimons receives your IP address and your browser's User-Agent. Each request's Origin header also names Eddy's extension ID, which tells Voltimons the request came from Eddy. Eddy adds nothing else: no cookies, credentials, referrer, item IDs, page addresses, Vortex user IDs or account data.

A refused request still sends this. When last checked (10 October 2026), Voltimons' API accepted requests only from its own website. While Market data is on, Eddy still sends its requests (at most every 6 hours once failures add up), but the browser blocks the replies, so Eddy gets no data.

On Vortex pages

Eddy reads only what it needs to place its values and settings, and sends none of it anywhere:

  • Catalog: each card's item ID, confirmed by the card's own link, and whether the card is marked limited.
  • Profiles: the item IDs in the links of the items the profile lists as worn (for Avatar Value), and the user ID in the page address (only for the Voltimons player link below, while Market data is on).
  • Settings: the structure of the tab list, panel and account menu (their class names), to add Eddy's tab and its “Eddy settings” entry.
  • Item pages: nothing. Eddy adds nothing there.
  • Every page: the site's light or dark theme.

It never reads names, prices, sellers, account details, balances or inventories; never reads cookies, page storage, session data or tokens; and never calls Vortex's endpoints. It adds its own elements to the page and, only when you use its filters, reorders or hides catalog cards, restoring them when you clear the filters.

Voltimons player link. Only while Market data is on, the Limited RAP entry on a Vortex profile links to that player's page on Voltimons, using the user ID from the profile's address. Eddy never requests that page. If you click the link, your browser opens it in a new tab with no referrer or opener, and Voltimons receives the address, including that public user ID, along with what any page you open sends, such as your IP address.

This website

No tracking

No analytics, cookies, third-party scripts or web-font services. The font is served from this site.

Two preferences, in your browser

The theme and the sidebar state are remembered in this browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

Optional Ko-fi link

The “Support Eddy on Ko-fi” link in the footer is a plain link. Nothing is loaded from Ko-fi until you click it; it opens ko-fi.com in a new tab without a referrer, and Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies there.
