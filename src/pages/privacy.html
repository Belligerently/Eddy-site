Privacy

Eddy collects nothing about you. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, accounts, backend or advertising.

Permissions the extension asks for

storage

Eddy's settings and, only after you turn on Market data, the downloaded market-data cache. Kept on this device; not synced.

Access to playvortex.io

To add Eddy's settings tab and its values on catalog and profile pages. Browsers list this as access to that site.

Nothing else

No host permissions, and no tabs, cookies, scripting, webRequest or identity.

Network requests

While Market data (Voltimons) is off, the default, Eddy makes no requests at all.

While it is on, Eddy's background worker requests exactly two addresses, https://api.voltimons.io/items and https://api.voltimons.io/stats, at most every 15 minutes. The requests carry no cookies, credentials, referrer, item IDs, page addresses or anything about you. The extension's security policy blocks every other connection.

On Vortex pages

Eddy reads only item IDs, the limited-item marker and the site's light or dark theme, to place its values.

  • It never reads names, prices, sellers, accounts, balances or inventories.
  • It never reads cookies, page storage, session data or tokens.
  • It never calls Vortex's endpoints.

This website

No tracking

No analytics, cookies, third-party scripts or web-font services. The font is served from this site.

Two preferences, in your browser

The theme and the sidebar state are remembered in this browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

Optional Ko-fi link

The “Support Eddy on Ko-fi” link in the footer is a plain link. Nothing is loaded from Ko-fi until you click it; it opens ko-fi.com in a new tab without a referrer, and Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies there.
