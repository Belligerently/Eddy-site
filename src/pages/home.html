What Eddy does

In the current build

{{icon:values}}Catalog worth{{icon:arrow}}

On Vortex's catalog, limited items get a small Worth line from Voltimons' unofficial market data, with sorting and value filters. Off until you turn on Market data.

{{icon:features}}Avatar Value{{icon:arrow}}

On profiles, the combined worth of the limited items a profile lists as worn. It is not an inventory or account value.

{{icon:popup}}Popup and settings{{icon:arrow}}

A toolbar popup to search and sort estimated values, and an Eddy tab inside Vortex's own Settings page.

Honest by design

{{icon:info}}Clearly labelled

Estimates are always in italics and never shown as RAP or an official price. Missing values say so in words, never as a made-up number.

{{icon:privacy}}Reads only item IDs

On Vortex pages Eddy reads item IDs and nothing else: no names, prices, accounts, cookies or inventories. Privacy details

{{icon:check}}Off by default

Market data is off until you turn it on. While it is off, Eddy makes no network requests at all.
